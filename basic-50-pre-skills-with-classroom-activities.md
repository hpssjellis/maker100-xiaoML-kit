# Maker100: 50 Classroom-Ready Prerequisite Skills

| # | Category | Skill Description | In-Class Example Assignment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Digital** | Install/Launch Software | Open the Arduino IDE on the laptop and verify the "Blink" sketch is visible. |
| 2 | **Digital** | Text Manipulation | Copy a block of code from a website and paste it into a blank Notepad/Text file. |
| 3 | **Digital** | Version Control awareness | Rename a file on the desktop from `project.ino` to `project_v01_initials.ino`. |
| 4 | **Digital** | Port Identification | Plug in the XIAO board and find the new "COM Port" in the IDE Tools menu. |
| 5 | **Hardware** | Visual Identification | Point to the Camera, Microphone, and Reset button on a XIAO Sense board. |
| 6 | **Hardware** | Connector Safety | Gently plug and unplug a USB-C cable 5 times without "wiggling" the port. |
| 7 | **Hardware** | Pin Counting | Identify and count the physical pins on the XIAO from D0 to D10. |
| 8 | **Math** | Equation Solving | Solve: If an LED needs 2V and you have a 5V battery, how many Volts must the resistor block? |
| 9 | **Math** | Scaling/Mapping | If a dial goes from 0 to 1023, write down the number that represents roughly 25%. |
| 10 | **Math** | Metric Measurement | Use a ruler to measure the XIAO board's width in millimeters. |
| 11 | **Logic** | Boolean Logic | Given a pile of LEDs, separate them into "Red" AND "Not Red." |
| 12 | **Logic** | Conditional Branching | Write a "Human Script": "IF it is 10:00 AM, THEN stand up, ELSE stay seated." |
| 13 | **Logic** | Iteration (Loops) | Write "I will check the sensor" 5 times on a piece of paper using a tally mark system. |
| 14 | **Circuits** | Polarity Check | Look at an LED and identify the Anode (long leg) and Cathode (short leg). |
| 15 | **Circuits** | Pathfinding | Trace a path on a breadboard with a finger to show how Row 5 is connected. |
| 16 | **Circuits** | Continuity Sketching | Draw a single-line path from a battery to a light and back to the battery. |
| 17 | **Electronics**| Component Symbolism | Draw the schematic symbol for a Resistor (zigzag) and an LED (arrow/triangle). |
| 18 | **Electronics**| Voltage Awareness | Look at a battery and write down its specific Voltage rating printed on the side. |
| 19 | **Software** | Syntax Error Hunting | On a printed sheet of code, circle the missing semicolon at the end of a line. |
| 20 | **Software** | Case Sensitivity | Circle the difference between the words `Digitalwrite` and `digitalWrite`. |
| 21 | **Physics** | Force Application | Press a tactile button; describe the "click" feeling when the circuit closes. |
| 22 | **Physics** | Gravity/Balance | Balance a XIAO board on its edge to understand its center of mass for builds. |
| 23 | **Design** | Layout Sketching | Draw a 1:1 scale box on paper where you think the battery and MCU should sit. |
| 24 | **Design** | Labeling | Annotate a sketch of a button with the labels "Input" and "Power." |
| 25 | **Comms** | Peer Review | Explain to a neighbor how an LED works; have them repeat it back to you. |
| 26 | **Comms** | Status Reporting | Write a 1-sentence "Log Entry" of what you accomplished in the last 10 minutes. |
| 27 | **Safety** | Identifying Hazards | Point to the "Caution" or "Danger" symbols on a Lithium Polymer (LiPo) battery. |
| 28 | **Safety** | Workspace Tidying | Clear all non-essential items (water, bags) from the "Soldering Zone." |
| 29 | **Organization**| Kit Management | Open your XIAO-kit and verify every item on the packing list is present. |
| 30 | **Organization**| Small Parts Handling | Use tweezers to move 5 tiny resistors from one bin to another without dropping them. |
| 31 | **ML Concepts** | Data Quality | Take two photos of a pen: one "Blurry" and one "Clear." Explain which is better for AI. |
| 32 | **ML Concepts** | Classification | Group 10 classroom objects into "Plastic," "Metal," or "Wood" categories. |
| 33 | **ML Concepts** | Feature Detection | List 3 visual features that make a "Chair" different from a "Table." |
| 34 | **ML Concepts** | Trigger Words | Clap your hands twice; explain how an AI might distinguish that from a voice. |
| 35 | **Media** | Camera Framing | Hold the XIAO camera 10cm from a page; verify the text is legible on the screen. |
| 36 | **Media** | Background Noise | Sit in silence for 30 seconds and list 3 "background sounds" the mic might hear. |
| 37 | **Prototyping** | Rigid Structures | Fold a piece of paper into a "U" shape to show how it becomes stronger. |
| 38 | **Prototyping** | Friction Fit | Slide a pencil into a cardboard hole so it stays without using glue or tape. |
| 39 | **Input** | Range of Motion | Rotate a potentiometer (knob) and identify its "Start" and "Stop" points. |
| 40 | **Output** | Frequency Check | Tap your desk at 1 beat per second (1Hz) to simulate a blinking LED. |
| 41 | **IoT** | Signal Obstruction | Place your hand over a WiFi antenna; predict if the signal gets better or worse. |
| 42 | **IoT** | Addressing | Write down the unique "MAC Address" or ID printed on your XIAO or RAK board. |
| 43 | **Resilience** | Search Proficiency | Use a computer to find the "Pinout Diagram" for the XIAO ESP32S3. |
| 44 | **Resilience** | Re-reading | Read a 4-step instruction set; summarize it to the teacher before starting. |
| 45 | **Tooling** | Stripping Wire | Use a wire stripper to remove exactly 5mm of insulation from a jumper wire. |
| 46 | **Tooling** | Adhesion | Use a small piece of "Blue Tack" or tape to temporarily mount a sensor. |
| 47 | **Spatial** | 3D Visualization | Look at a top-down drawing of a kit; point to where the USB port is. |
| 48 | **Ethics** | Consent | Ask a classmate for permission before taking a photo of their project. |
| 49 | **Ethics** | Resource Sharing | Share a single multimeter with another group by timing your measurements. |
| 50 | **Curiosity** | Investigative Draw | Sketch the inside of your battery compartment (where the springs and tabs are). |
