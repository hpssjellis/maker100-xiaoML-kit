# Maker100: 50 Prerequisite Skills with Practice Assignments

| # | Category | Skill Description | Example Assignment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Digital** | Install and launch software. | Download the Arduino IDE and open the "Blink" example sketch. |
| 2 | **Digital** | Extract/Unzip compressed files. | Download a .zip folder of images and extract them to a new folder. |
| 3 | **Digital** | Text-based search in documents. | Open a 20-page PDF and use `Ctrl+F` to find the word "Voltage." |
| 4 | **Digital** | Screen Capturing. | Take a screenshot of an error message and crop it to show only the text. |
| 5 | **Hardware** | Visual Cable Identification. | Find three different USB cables in your house and label them (A, C, Micro). |
| 6 | **Hardware** | Continuity Visualization. | Draw a circle on paper; explain why a "break" in the line stops flow. |
| 7 | **Hardware** | Manual Dexterity. | Strip the plastic insulation off the end of a scrap wire using pliers. |
| 8 | **Math** | Algebra Substitution. | If $V = I \times R$, find $V$ when $I=0.02$ and $R=220$. |
| 9 | **Math** | Measurement Conversion. | Use a ruler to measure a credit card in centimeters and convert to mm. |
| 10 | **Math** | Threshold Logic. | Write down which numbers are > 75 in a list of 10 random values. |
| 11 | **Logic** | Flowcharting. | Draw a chart for "How to make toast" using boxes and arrows. |
| 12 | **Logic** | Sequence Execution. | Follow a Lego instruction manual or a complex origami diagram. |
| 13 | **Logic** | Boolean Sorting. | Take 10 household items and sort them into "Electronic" or "Non-Electronic." |
| 14 | **Circuits** | Component Identification. | Look at a circuit board and point out a resistor vs. a battery. |
| 15 | **Circuits** | Battery Orientation. | Correctly insert AA batteries into a device looking at the + / - signs. |
| 16 | **Electronics**| LED Polarity Check. | Find the long leg (+) and short leg (-) of an LED. |
| 17 | **Electronics**| Breadboard Pathing. | Draw a line showing how electricity travels inside a breadboard row. |
| 18 | **Internet** | Forum Navigation. | Find a thread on a site like Reddit or StackOverflow solving a tech bug. |
| 19 | **Internet** | Hotspot Connectivity. | Enable a "Personal Hotspot" on a phone and connect a laptop to it. |
| 20 | **Physics** | Torque Observation. | Try to open a door by pushing near the hinge vs. near the handle. |
| 21 | **Physics** | Vibration Sensing. | Place a hand on a speaker; describe how the "data" feels physically. |
| 22 | **Physics** | Light Reflection. | Use a mirror to bounce a flashlight beam onto a specific target. |
| 23 | **Design** | Concept Sketching. | Draw a diagram of a battery connected to a lightbulb and a switch. |
| 24 | **Design** | Dimensioning. | Draw a box and label the length, width, and height in millimeters. |
| 25 | **Comms** | Technical Writing. | Write a 3-sentence email explaining exactly why a website won't load. |
| 26 | **Comms** | Photography. | Take a "Macro" photo of a small object (like a coin) that is perfectly in focus. |
| 27 | **Safety** | Heat Awareness. | Identify the specific parts of a glue gun that should never be touched. |
| 28 | **Safety** | Tool Inspection. | Check a power cord for frays or exposed wires before plugging it in. |
| 29 | **Organization**| Inventory Management. | Count and group 20 different screws by their length and head type. |
| 30 | **Organization**| File Naming. | Rename 5 photos to `Project_Step1.jpg`, `Project_Step2.jpg`, etc. |
| 31 | **Software** | Port Selection. | Plug in a mouse and find its name in the computer's "Device Manager." |
| 32 | **Software** | Syntax Recognition. | Spot the difference between `digitalWrite` and `digitalwrite`. |
| 33 | **ML Concepts** | Data Labeling. | Sort 20 photos of fruit into folders named "Apple" and "Banana." |
| 34 | **ML Concepts** | Pattern Recognition. | Look at a "Captcha" and identify all squares containing a "traffic light." |
| 35 | **Media** | Video Trimming. | Record a 60-second video and trim it down to the "best" 10 seconds. |
| 36 | **Media** | Audio Levels. | Record a voice memo from 1 inch away vs 3 feet away; compare clarity. |
| 37 | **Prototyping** | Cardboard Modeling. | Build a stand for your phone using only a cereal box and tape. |
| 38 | **Prototyping** | Fastening. | Connect two pieces of cardboard using a "tab and slot" method (no tape). |
| 39 | **Input** | Switch Testing. | Use a multimeter (or a battery/LED) to see if a switch is "On" or "Off." |
| 40 | **Output** | Signal Interpretation. | Count the number of times a car's blinker flashes in 10 seconds. |
| 41 | **CAD** | 3D Visualization. | Use Tinkercad (free) to place a cube on top of a cylinder. |
| 42 | **Robotics** | Loop Observation. | Watch a microwave timer; describe the "loop" it follows every second. |
| 43 | **IoT** | App Pairing. | Pair a set of Bluetooth headphones to a new device successfully. |
| 44 | **Ethics** | Source Attribution. | Copy a quote from a website and paste the URL as a "Source." |
| 45 | **Math** | Mapping Scales. | If a sensor reads 0-100, what is the value when it is exactly half full? |
| 46 | **Resilience** | Error Logs. | Intentionally misspell a URL and find the "404 Error" page. |
| 47 | **Resilience** | Instruction Reading. | Assemble a piece of flat-pack furniture or a small toy kit by the book. |
| 48 | **Spatial** | Perspective Drawing. | Draw a 3D cube showing three of its faces (top, front, side). |
| 49 | **Tooling** | Cable Management. | Use a twist-tie or rubber band to neatly coil a laptop charger cable. |
| 50 | **Curiosity** | Teardown. | (Safe) Take the battery cover off a remote; draw how the springs touch the cells. |
